Honey Cake

Honey Cake was founded with a simple dream — to bring warmth, sweetness, and happiness into every celebration. It started as a small home bakery in Phnom Penh, where the aroma of freshly baked cakes filled the kitchen each morning. Our founder believed that every cake should be made with love, care, and a touch of natural sweetness — just like honey.

What began as a few homemade cakes shared with friends soon grew into a beloved local brand. Customers fell in love with our soft textures, rich flavors, and beautiful designs. Today, Honey Cake is known for crafting custom cakes for birthdays, weddings, and special occasions — all made from the finest ingredients and a passion for perfection.
